Smaller points, not drawn

  • «Hopp over» on the first onboarding step should skip only steps 2 and 3. The household drives every number.
  • Leave the onboarding water field empty and focused instead of filling in one day's worth.
  • Rapport: say why «Lag PDF og del» is greyed out when no rooms have things in them.
  • Nødinfo: «virker uten nett» is true for the data, but calls still need mobile coverage.
  • Pick one name each for Lager, beredskapslageret, DSBs liste and Utstyr.
